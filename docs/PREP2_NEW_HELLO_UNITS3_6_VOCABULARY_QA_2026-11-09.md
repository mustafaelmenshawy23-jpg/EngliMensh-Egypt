## Unit 5 — Helping Others
- 25 headwords; no duplicate headword found.
- Quiz answers reviewed; no definite wrong bold-marked answer was confirmed.

## Unit 6 — Environment
- 25 headwords; no duplicate headword found.
- Two quiz items need repair:
  - Q1: "Global warming is causing major changes in the earth's .......... climate." The marked choice is `global`, producing the awkward phrase "the earth's global climate" and not a clean test of the bank headword `climate`. Rewrite the sentence and options so the intended word is tested unambiguously.
  - Q15: "Climbing Mount Sinai is an amazing experience for adventurous tourists." The marked option is `Mountain`, but the sentence contains no blank. As written, the option cannot be inserted; add a proper blank and adjust capitalization/word form.
- The bank definition of `renewable` should be phrased as an adjective ("naturally replenished") and distinguished from the phrase `renewable energy`.
