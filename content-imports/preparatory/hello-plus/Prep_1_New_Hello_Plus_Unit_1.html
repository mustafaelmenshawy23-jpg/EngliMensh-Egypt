Unit 1: Stative vs. Action Verbs & Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

1. Stative Verbs vs. Action Verbs (أفعال الحالة وأفعال الحركة)

Action (Dynamic) Verbs: Describe physical actions. Can be used in both simple and continuous tenses (run, write, fix, build → He is fixing the car now.)

Stative (State) Verbs: Describe emotions, thoughts, senses, and possession. NEVER used in continuous (-ing) forms, even with 'now' or 'at the moment'!

  • Emotions: love, like, hate, prefer, want, need. (e.g., I want water now. NOT: I am wanting.)
  • Thoughts & Mental States: know, understand, believe, remember, think (opinion), mean. (e.g., She understands the rule now.)
  • Possession: have, own, belong, possess. (e.g., This pen belongs to me.)
  • Senses: see, hear, smell, taste, seem, appear. (e.g., The food tastes delicious.)

2. Present Simple vs. Present Continuous Contrast

Present Simple (Habits, Permanent States, Facts)Present Continuous (Temporary Actions Right Now)
The mechanic repairs cars every day.He is repairing this car at the moment.
Veterinarians love animals. (Stative verb)The vet is examining the puppy now. (Action verb)
