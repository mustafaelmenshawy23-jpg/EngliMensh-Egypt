Unit 6: Indefinite Pronouns (Some-, Any-, Every-, No-) & Advanced Quantifiers

1. Indefinite Pronouns (الضمائر غير المحددة)

CategoryPeople (الأشخاص)Things (الأشياء)Places (الأماكن)
Some- (Affirmative)someone / somebodysomethingsomewhere
Any- (Negative & Questions)anyone / anybodyanythinganywhere
Every- (Universal/All)everyone / everybodyeverythingeverywhere
No- (Negative Meaning)no one / nobodynothingnowhere

*Critical Rule:* All indefinite pronouns are grammatically SINGULAR and take singular verbs! (e.g., Everyone is happy. Somebody has lost a bag.)

2. Advanced Quantifiers (a few, a little, few, little)

  • a few: Small number of countable nouns (enough) → I have a few friends.
  • few: Very small number of countable nouns (not enough / scarce) → He has few coins.
  • a little: Small amount of uncountable nouns (enough) → Add a little sugar.
  • little: Very small amount of uncountable nouns (not enough / scarce) → There is little water left.
