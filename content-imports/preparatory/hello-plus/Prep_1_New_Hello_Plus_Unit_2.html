Unit 2: Modals of Obligation, Prohibition, & Permission (Must, Have to, Can)

1. Modals of Obligation & Necessity (must, have to, need to)

  • Must + base verb: Strong internal personal obligation or vital rule → I must study hard. Drivers must stop at red lights.
  • Have to / Has to + base verb: External obligation dictated by laws, school regulations, or circumstances → Students have to wear uniform.

2. Prohibition vs. Lack of Necessity vs. Permission

Modal VerbMeaning (المعنى والقاعدة)Example
Mustn't + infProhibition / Forbidden (ممنوع ومحرم بقانون)You mustn't use phones during exams.
Don't/Doesn't have to + infLack of necessity (غير مضطر - ليس ضرورياً)Tomorrow is Friday; I don't have to get up early.
Can / Allowed to + infPermission (مسموح به قانونياً)We can borrow three books from the library.
