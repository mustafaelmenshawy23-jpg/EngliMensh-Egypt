Unit 3: Defining Relative Clauses (Who, Which, That, Where, Whose)

Defining Relative Clauses (ضمائر الوصل المحددة)

Relative PronounUsed For (الاستخدام)Example
who / thatPeople (الأشخاص والفاعل/المفعول العاقل)A scientist is someone who conducts experiments.
which / thatThings & Animals (غير العاقل والأشياء والحيوانات)A microscope is a tool which magnifies tiny cells.
wherePlaces (الأماكن - حيث يحدث فعل)A laboratory is a room where scientists work.
whosePossession (الملكية - يحل محل صفات الملكية)That is the boy whose invention won the prize.

*Notice:* No commas are used in defining relative clauses because the clause is essential to the sentence's core meaning.
