Unit 5: Zero & First Conditionals (If / When / Unless)

1. Zero Conditional (الحالة الشرطية الصفرية - حقائق علمية وعامة)

Rule: If / When + Present Simple, Present Simple

• If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.

• When glaciers melt, sea levels rise.

2. First Conditional (الحالة الشرطية الأولى - احتمالات وتوقعات مستقبلية)

Rule: If / Unless + Present Simple, will / won't + Infinitive

• If we recycle plastic, we will reduce environmental pollution.

• If it rains heavily tomorrow, we won't go camping.

• Unless (= If not): Unless we protect endangered species, they will become extinct.
