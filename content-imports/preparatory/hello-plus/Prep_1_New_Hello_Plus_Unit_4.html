Unit 4: Past Continuous vs. Past Simple (While, As, Just as, When)

Past Continuous vs. Past Simple (الماضي المستمر والماضي البسيط)

Past Continuous (was / were + v-ing): Used for long background actions in progress in the past.

Past Simple: Used for short, sudden actions that interrupted the long action.

Time ConjunctionRule StructureExample
While / As / Just asWhile + Past Continuous, Past SimpleWhile we were excavating the tomb, we uncovered a statue.
WhenWhen + Past Simple, Past ContinuousWhen the lights went out, I was reading papyrus texts.
Two parallel past actions (While)While + Past Continuous, Past ContinuousWhile Mona was studying, her brother was painting.
