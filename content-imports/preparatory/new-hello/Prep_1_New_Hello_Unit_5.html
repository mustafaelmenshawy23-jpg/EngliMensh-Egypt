Unit 5: The Past Simple Tense (Regular & Irregular Verbs, Was / Were)

1. The Past Simple Tense (زمن الماضي البسيط)

Usage: Actions completed in the past at a specific finished time (yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago, once).

CategoryAffirmative FormNegative (didn't + inf)Question Form (Did + S + inf?)
Regular Verbs (+d, +ed, +ied)We climbed a mountain.
They visited Luxor.		We didn't climb the cliff.
He didn't visit the museum.		Did you visit the citadel?
→ Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
Irregular Verbs (حفظ بدون قاعدة)I saw a waterfall.
She bought souvenirs.		I didn't see any caves.
She didn't buy a postcard.		Did they go camping?
→ Yes, they did. / No, they didn't.
Verb to Be in Past (was / were)I/He/She/It was happy.
We/You/They were tired.		He wasn't ill.
They weren't late.		Was it sunny? → Yes, it was.
Were they at the beach?
