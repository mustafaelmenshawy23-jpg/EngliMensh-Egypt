Unit 4: The Present Continuous Tense & The Imperative

1. The Present Continuous Tense (زمن المضارع المستمر)

Usage: Actions happening right now at the moment of speaking (now, look, listen, at the moment, at present).

SubjectAffirmative (am/is/are + v-ing)NegativeQuestion Form
II am ('m) sending a text.I am not sendingAm I doing this right?
He / She / ItShe is ('s) taking a selfie.He isn't clickingIs he using his laptop?
We / You / TheyThey are ('re) charging phones.We aren't callingAre you reading online?

Spelling Rules for -ing: drop silent -e (take → taking), double consonant after short vowel (tap → tapping, run → running).

2. The Imperative (صيغة الأمر والتعليمات)

• Affirmative Imperative: Base verb without subject → Click on this icon. Keep your password secret.

• Negative Imperative: Don't + Infinitive → Don't send photos to strangers. Don't make friends with bad people online.
