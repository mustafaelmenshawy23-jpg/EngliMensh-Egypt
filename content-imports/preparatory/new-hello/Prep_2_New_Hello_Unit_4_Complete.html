3. Grammar Guide: Modals of Obligation (Must / Mustn't)

1. Must: Used to express strong obligation, rules, and laws (things that are necessary to do).

  • Structure: Subject + must + base verb (infinitive).
  • Example: You must respect school rules.

2. Mustn't: Used to express prohibition (things that are forbidden or not allowed).

  • Structure: Subject + mustn't + base verb (infinitive).
  • Example: You mustn't drop litter in the park.
