Unit 3: Have got / Has got & Describing Appearance / Personality

1. Have got / Has got (فعل يملك / لديه)

SubjectAffirmative (يملك)Negative (لا يملك)Question Form (هل يملك؟)
I / We / You / They / Pluralhave got ('ve got)
They have got dark hair.		haven't got
I haven't got glasses.		Have you got a car?
→ Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
He / She / It / Singularhas got ('s got)
He has got a beard.		hasn't got
She hasn't got curly hair.		Has he got a moustache?
→ Yes, he has. / No, he hasn't.

Wh- Question: What has / have + Subject + got? → What has Salah got? → He has got curly hair and a beard.

2. Physical Appearance vs. Personality Adjectives

• Appearance: tall, short, curly hair, straight hair, dark hair, blonde hair, beard, moustache, glasses.

• Personality & Character: kind, friendly, clever, funny, brave, helpful.
