Unit 6: Countable & Uncountable Nouns, Some/Any, Much/Many, A lot of, Enough

1. Countable vs. Uncountable Nouns (الأسماء المعدودة وغير المعدودة)

  • Countable Nouns: Can be counted, have singular and plural forms (a raisin → raisins, a nut → nuts, an egg → eggs).
  • Uncountable Nouns: Cannot be counted, have NO plural, always treated as singular (rice, sugar, milk, water, salt, tea, cheese).
