Unit 2: Telling Time, Can/Can't for Ability, & Possessive ('s)

1. Telling the Time (الإخبار عن الوقت)

• O'clock: It is eight o'clock. (8:00)

• Quarter past: It is quarter past eight. (8:15)

• Half past: It is half past eight. (8:30)

• Quarter to: It is quarter to nine. (8:45)

2. Ability & Inability (Can / Can't + Infinitive)

Form: Subject + can / can't + Base Verb (المصدر)

• Affirmative: She can speak two foreign languages fluently.

• Negative: He can't paddle a heavy canoe alone.

• Question: Can you solve this equation? → Yes, I can. / No, I can't.

3. Possessive 's & s' (الملكية باستخدام الفاصلة العليا)

• Singular Noun: Add 's → the teacher's desk, Mona's pencil case.

• Regular Plural Noun: Add only ' → the boys' playground, the students' timetable.

• Irregular Plural: Add 's → the children's books, the women's clothes.
