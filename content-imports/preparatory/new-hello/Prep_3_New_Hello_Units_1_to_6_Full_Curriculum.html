Around Town

🖼️ المفردات المصورة والتعاريف الشاملة 25 كلمة

اضغط على أيقونة الصوت 🔊 للاستماع للنطق الصحيح

aquarium

noun

متحف الأحياء المائية

Def: A public park or building where live water animals and plants are kept.

"You can see beautiful sea creatures at the aquarium."

botanical gardens

noun

حدائق نباتية

Def: A large garden where trees, flowers, and plants are grown for scientific study.

"We walked through the peaceful botanical gardens yesterday."

monument

noun

أثر تاريخي

Def: A building, statue, or structure built to remind people of a famous person or event.

"The Sphinx is a famous ancient monument in Egypt."

lake

noun

بحيرة

Def: A large area of water surrounded by land.

"Lake Timsah is a beautiful spot near Ismailia."

library

noun

مكتبة للقراءة والتصوير

Def: A place where books, newspapers, and magazines are kept for people to read or borrow.

"I borrowed two interesting books from the local library."

sports centre

noun

مركز رياضي

Def: A building where people can play different sports and exercise.

"My brother plays handball at the sports centre every Tuesday."

traffic

noun

حركة المرور

Def: The cars, trucks, and buses using a road.

"The traffic is very slow during the morning rush hour."

pollution

noun

التلوث

Def: Damage caused to water, air, or land by harmful substances.

"Electric buses help reduce air pollution in big cities."

peaceful

adj

هادئ / خالي من الضوضاء

Def: Quiet and calm, without any worry or noise.

"Living in the countryside is very peaceful and refreshing."

passenger

noun

راكب / مسافر

Def: A person who is traveling in a vehicle, train, or plane, but not driving it.

"The train carried over five hundred passengers to Cairo."

ferry

noun

معدية / معدّية نهرية

Def: A boat that carries people and vehicles across a river or a short stretch of sea.

"We took a ferry to cross the Nile River to the east bank."

tram

noun

ترام / قطار المدينة الشارعي

Def: An electric vehicle that runs along metal rails in city streets.

"Alexandria is famous for its historic blue tram."

metro

noun

مترو الأنفاق

Def: An underground electric railway system in a city.

"Taking the metro is the fastest way to travel across Cairo."

platform

noun

رصيف القطار

Def: The area next to the railway tracks where passengers get on or off a train.

"The express train to Luxor leaves from platform number four."

timetable

noun

جدول المواعيد

Def: A list showing the times when trains, buses, or planes arrive and depart.

"Check the train timetable before planning your travel."

exhibition

noun

معرض (فني/تاريخي)

Def: A public show where objects such as paintings or historical items are displayed.

"We visited a space exhibition at the science museum."

map

noun

خريطة

Def: A drawing of an area showing roads, rivers, cities, and landmarks.

"Follow the tourist map to find the museum easily."

directions

noun

اتجاهات السير

Def: Instructions telling someone how to get to a specific place.

"Could you please give me directions to the nearest bank?"

turn right

phrase

اتجه يميناً

Def: To change direction towards your right hand side.

"Go past the library and turn right at the corner."

go straight on

phrase

سِر للأمام مباشرة

Def: To move continuously in a forward direction without turning.

"Go straight on for 200 meters until you reach the park."

corner

noun

ناصية / ناصية الشارع

Def: The place where two streets or roads meet.

"The bakery is right on the corner of Market Street."

opposite

prep

مقابل / مواجه لـ

Def: Facing someone or something, on the other side of a street or area.

"The school is located directly opposite the big hospital."

natural reserve

noun

محمية طبيعية

Def: A protected area for plants and animals to live safely.

"Ras Mohamed is a famous natural reserve in Egypt."

outdoors

adv

في الهواء الطلق / بالخارج

Def: In the open air, outside of buildings.

"Children love playing sports outdoors when it is sunny."

crossword

noun

كلمات متقاطعة

Def: A word puzzle where you write words in squares following clues.

"Grandfather enjoys solving the daily crossword in the newspaper."
