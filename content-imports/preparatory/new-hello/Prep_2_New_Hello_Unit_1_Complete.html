3. Grammar: Present Simple Tense (Detailed Explanation)

Form (التكوين):
- With (I, We, They, You) and plural nouns: Base verb (Infinitive without 'to').
- With (He, She, It) and singular nouns: Verb + s / es / ies.
Usage (الاستخدام):
- Habits and daily routines.
- Permanent facts and truths.
Keywords (الكلمات الدالة):
- Always, usually, often, sometimes, never (Adverbs of frequency come before the main verb, but after verb 'to be').

5. Reading Comprehension

Passage: Daily routines are essential for success. Tarek is a Prep 2 student who organizes his schedule every week. He wakes up early at 6:00 AM, has a healthy breakfast, and walks to school. After school, Tarek practices football and reviews his lessons. By managing his time well, he achieves his goals and stays energetic.

1. What grade is Tarek in?
Answer: Prep 2
2. What time does Tarek wake up?
Answer: At 6:00 AM
3. What sport does Tarek practice after school?
Answer: Football
4. Why are daily routines essential according to the passage?
Answer: For success
5. How does Tarek achieve his goals?
Answer: By managing his time well
