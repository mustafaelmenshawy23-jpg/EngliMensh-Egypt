3. Grammar Guide: External Obligation (Have to / Don't have to)

1. Have to / Has to: Used to express external obligation, rules imposed by others, or laws (it is necessary).

  • Affirmative: I / We / They / You + have to + base verb. | He / She / It + has to + base verb.
  • Example: Students have to wear uniforms at school.

2. Don't have to / Doesn't have to: Used to express lack of obligation (something is not necessary, but you can do it if you want).

  • Negative: I / We / They / You + don't have to + base verb. | He / She / It + doesn't have to + base verb.
  • Example: Today is a holiday; we don't have to go to school.
