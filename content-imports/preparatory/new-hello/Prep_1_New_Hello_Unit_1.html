Unit 1: Present Simple Tense & Possessive Pronouns / Adjectives

1. The Present Simple Tense (زمن المضارع البسيط)

Primary Usages (الاستخدامات): Habits, daily routines, general facts, and permanent states.

SubjectAffirmative VerbNegative FormQuestion Form
I / We / You / They / PluralBase Form (play)don't + infinitive (don't play)Do + S + inf ...?
He / She / It / SingularVerb + -s / -es / -ies (plays)doesn't + infinitive (doesn't play)Does + S + inf ...?

Spelling with He/She/It: Add -es after -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o (watches, goes). Consonant + y → -ies (studies). Vowel + y → -s (plays).

Adverbs of Frequency (always, usually, often, sometimes, never): Placed BEFORE main verbs (He always arrives on time), but AFTER verb to be (She is never late).

2. Possessive Adjectives vs. Possessive Pronouns (صفات وضمائر الملكية)

Subject PronounPossessive Adjective (+ Noun)Possessive Pronoun (Stands Alone)
IThis is my pen.This pen is mine.
HeThat is his car.That car is his.
SheIt is her bag.It is hers.
WeThese are our books.These books are ours.
TheyThose are their chairs.Those chairs are theirs.
YouIs that your bike?Is that bike yours?

Apostrophe 's for Possession: Singular noun: the boy's toy. Regular plural: the girls' school. Irregular plural: the children's room.
