3. Grammar: Past Simple Tense (Detailed Explanation)

Form (التكوين):
- Regular verbs: Verb + ed / d / ied (e.g., worked, played, studied).
- Irregular verbs: Memorized forms (e.g., go -> went, see -> saw, do -> did).
Usage (الاستخدام):
- Actions that happened and finished in the past.
Keywords (الكلمات الدالة):
- Yesterday, ago, last (week/month/year), in 2020, once.
