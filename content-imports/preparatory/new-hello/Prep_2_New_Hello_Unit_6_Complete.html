3. Grammar Guide: Comparative & Superlative Adjectives

1. Comparative Adjectives (مقارنة بين اثنين): Used to compare two things or people.

  • Short adjectives: adjective + -er + than. (e.g., Tall ➔ taller than)
  • Long adjectives: more + adjective + than. (e.g., Beautiful ➔ more beautiful than)

2. Superlative Adjectives (التفضيل بين أكثر من اثنين): Used to compare one thing with a whole group.

  • Short adjectives: the + adjective + -est. (e.g., Tall ➔ the tallest)
  • Long adjectives: the most + adjective. (e.g., Beautiful ➔ the most beautiful)

Irregular Adjectives: Good ➔ better than / the best | Bad ➔ worse than / the worst | Far ➔ farther than / the farthest.
